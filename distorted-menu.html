The story so far
The city was built to hold a single memory, and the memory won't stop growing. Streets fold into themselves. Towers you passed an hour ago are now standing where you were. Frag 01 — "I mapped the avenue three times today. It was longer each time." Frag 02 — "There are people here. They walk the same loop. They never look up." Frag 03 — "Something moves when I stop moving. It only follows what it can remember." Frag 04 — "You don't escape the distortion. You learn to steer it."
Your suit keeps you anchored to one true position. Everything else in the city is free to lie.
