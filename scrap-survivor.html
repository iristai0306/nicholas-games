HOW TO PLAY

🎯 You auto-fire at the nearest enemy — just focus on moving & dodging.
🕹️ Move: WASD / Arrows (PC) · joystick (mobile)
💨 Dash: SPACE (PC) · DASH button (mobile) — brief invincibility!
💰 Enemies drop scrap — grab it, spend it between waves
🛒 After each wave, the shop opens — buy upgrades & weapons
💀 A BOSS spawns every 5 waves. Big, angry, worth a lot.
🏆 Survive as many waves as you can. Your best is saved.
