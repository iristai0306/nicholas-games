How it works

1. Tap two specimens in your zoo.

2. Hit SPLICE. The baby inherits each trait from one parent.

3. Every trait has a 14% chance to mutate into something neither parent has.

4. More mutations = rarer + more valuable:

COMMON 0-1 UNCOMMON 2 RARE 3 LEGENDARY 4+

5. Zoo caps at 40. Release the boring ones to keep splicing.

6. Hold a specimen to inspect, rename, or release it.

Tip: two legendary parents won't make a legendary. Mutation is random — the only way to get one is to keep splicing. Legendary is roughly 1 in 60 births.
