How to Play 🐾

1 Pick a cat from the bottom bar, then tap a grass tile to place it. Cats shoot by themselves.

2 Mice march along the path toward your cat homes. If they get through, you lose a life ❤️.

3 Every mouse you pop gives fish 🐟. Spend fish on more cats and upgrades.

4 Tap a placed cat to upgrade it or sell it.

5 Hit START WAVE, survive all 5 waves, and win 🏆

Each world throws a different enemy at you. This is just the start…
