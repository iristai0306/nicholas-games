How to Play

You are the villagers. The nobles want your land. Make them pay.

🧑‍🌾 Your Villagers = Your Towers

🧑‍🌾Farmhand — cheap pitchfork brawler. Great tile-filler.
🏹Poacher — bow. Ranged single-target. Rewards good placement.
🛡️Militia — tanky spear wall, heavy hits, slow.
🧪Alchemist — lobs potions. Splash damage on groups.
🪨Catapult — long range, huge splash. Expensive.
🌾Farm — no weapon, but pays gold over time. Economy vs. defense is a real choice.

⚔️ Commands & Tricks

🗡️Your Hero — drag anywhere to run your crowned hero around. He auto-attacks and levels up as he fights.
✊RALLY — hit it when things get hairy: every pleb attacks twice as fast +25% damage for 6 seconds. 30s cooldown.
⏩Fast-forward — the ×1 button doubles game speed when you've got it under control.
👀Next — the HUD shows what's marching in on the next wave. Plan ahead!
🛡️Every noble that reaches your village costs a life — your villagers stay standing. Lose all lives and it's over.

⚔️ The Noble Army

🛡️Footman & Knight — the march. Knights are fast. 🏇Sheriff — armoured, tanky. 🐘War Elephant — mini-boss. 👑The King — wave 10. Survive him and PLEBANIA stands.
